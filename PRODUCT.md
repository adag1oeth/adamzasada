# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring leaders, product executives, founders, collaborators, and music-industry partners evaluating Adam Zasada's professional track record and creative identity.

## Product Purpose

Present one credible personal brand that leads with senior product leadership and gives Adam Rules a distinct, coherent chapter. Success means visitors quickly understand Adam's scope, trust the evidence, and can reach the relevant professional or music destination without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, independent, and creatively assured. Senior without corporate stiffness; expressive without self-inflation.

## Anti-references

Generic resume templates, startup glassmorphism, repetitive card grids, inflated LinkedIn language, cluttered link directories, autoplay media, and decorative effects that compete with the work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence, not claims.
- Keep one dominant action per section.
- Treat product and music as two chapters of one person.
- Use real photography and restrained motion to create character.
- Remove repetition before adding explanation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and links, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not autoplay audio or require third-party embeds to understand the page.
